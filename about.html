About Caddy

Caddy was founded to address isolation, stigma, and misinformation in women’s health. Our mission is to empower women by combining a sleek, modular healthcare kit with a dynamic digital platform that offers personalized guidance and a supportive community.

When you choose Caddy, you join a movement toward informed self-care and meaningful connection.
